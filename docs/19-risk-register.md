# Risk Register

## Scale

Probability (P) and impact (I) are 1–5. Score is `P × I`, used for triage rather than false precision. Owners are accountable roles until individuals are assigned. Residual scores are reassessed at phase gates.

## Review cadence

Critical risks (score 15+) are reviewed at each phase gate and monthly during active development. Triggered risks are reviewed immediately. Accepted risk requires scope, duration, approver, compensating control, and expiry. Closed risks retain evidence and may reopen.
