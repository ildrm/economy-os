2. Reproduce and remediate discovered high-priority security/PIT/data-integrity defects.
3. Implement governed behavioral theory/evidence/intervention and mathematical research capabilities; implement allocation profiles/plans and exact allocation simulation.
4. Integrate through authorized persistence/API and professional localized research workflows, preserving explicit missingness, provenance and simulation identity.
5. Exercise scientific boundaries, security, API, database, UI/accessibility, build and coverage gates. Review each discipline and fix discovered defects.
6. Reconcile status/roadmap/traceability with executable evidence. Record remaining scope gaps; do not promote roadmap phase acceptance.

All new research outputs must distinguish observation, candidate inference and simulation. A source span is evidence of source wording, not evidence that a mechanism caused an outcome. No user messages, deployment or external publishing are part of this execution.
