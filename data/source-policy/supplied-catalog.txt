**Grading:** `A` = official + free + machine-readable، `B` = official/free but with delay, manual files, registration, or licensing caveats، `C` = reputable third-party free tier، `D` = marketplace/listing data only.

> **Important for the AI:** `price`, `price_index`, `asking_price`, `transaction_price`, `reference_price`, and `MSRP` are different data types and must never be mixed without explicit conversion/normalization.
